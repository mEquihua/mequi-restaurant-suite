# Reports Module Requirements (Foundation Scope)

This document establishes the implementation requirements for the Restaurant Suite Reports module (Foundation scope). It defines the exact reporting endpoints to be built against the existing PostgreSQL read models, serving as a direct brief for the engineering team.

**Constraint on Scope**: Restaurant Suite reporting is explicitly NOT a BI platform. There is no generic query builder, analytical data warehouse, or backend charting engine. This module provides a small set of well-defined, parameterized REST endpoints returning aggregated JSON or CSV data from the primary database.

---

## 1. Foundation-Scope Reports

Each report maps to a discrete REST endpoint (e.g., `GET /api/v1/reports/sales/by-day`).

### 1.1 Sales by Day
*   **Correction to an earlier draft of this document**: grouping by `accounts.created_at` (when the tab was opened) is wrong — a visit opened at 23:58 and paid at 00:15 would land on the wrong business day. Group by when the sale actually completed instead.
*   **Reads tables**: `payments`, `accounts`, `locations`, `refunds`.
*   **Groups by**: The local date `DATE(payments.created_at AT TIME ZONE locations.timezone)`, using the earliest successful payment per account as that account's settlement moment for mixed-tender accounts with more than one payment row.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to` date range. Only include accounts with at least one successful payment; join to `accounts` for subtotal/tax/discount.
*   **Value definitions**:
    *   *Gross Sales*: `SUM(accounts.subtotal + accounts.tax)` for accounts settled that day.
    *   *Discounts*: `SUM(accounts.discount)`
    *   *Refunds*: `SUM(refunds.amount)` for refunds whose `payments.created_at` falls in the range (a refund is its own event and may land on a different day than the original sale — do not net it into the sale day it refunds).
    *   *Net Sales*: Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds.

### 1.2 Sales by Hour
*   **Reads tables**: `payments`, `accounts`, `locations`.
*   **Groups by**: The local hour of day `EXTRACT(HOUR FROM payments.created_at AT TIME ZONE locations.timezone)`, using the same settlement-moment logic as Sales by Day.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to` date range. Only include accounts with at least one successful payment.
*   **Value definitions**: Gross Sales and Net Sales (using the same calculations as Sales by Day).

### 1.3 Sales by Product
*   **Reads tables**: `order_lines`, `products`, `orders`, `locations`.
*   **Groups by**: `products.id`, `products.name`.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to` (based on `orders.created_at`). Exclude `order_lines.status IN ('VOIDED', 'CANCELLED', 'REJECTED')`.
*   **Pagination**: Requires `limit` and `cursor`/`offset` parameters, as product SKUs can be unbounded.
*   **Value definitions**:
    *   *Quantity Sold*: `SUM(order_lines.quantity)`
    *   *Gross Sales*: `SUM(order_lines.quantity * order_lines.unit_price)`

### 1.4 Sales by Category
*   **Reads tables**: `order_lines`, `products`, `categories`, `orders`, `locations`.
*   **Groups by**: `categories.id`, `categories.name`.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`. Exclude `order_lines.status IN ('VOIDED', 'CANCELLED', 'REJECTED')`.
*   **Value definitions**: Quantity Sold and Gross Sales, aggregated up to the category level.

### 1.5 Sales by Employee
*   **Reads tables**: `visits`, `staff`, `accounts`.
*   **Groups by**: `visits.staff_id`, `staff.first_name`, `staff.last_name`.
*   **Justification for `staff_id` selection**: We aggregate by the staff member who owned the visit/order (`visits.staff_id`), NOT the cashier who processed the payment. The server who owns the table is responsible for the upsell, guest experience, and tips. Attributing sales to the terminal cashier would artificially skew performance metrics for front-of-house staff.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to` (based on `visits.opened_at`).
*   **Value definitions**: Gross Sales and Net Sales generated by the server's visits, plus Total Tips collected on those visits.

### 1.6 Number of Orders & Average Ticket
*   **Reads tables**: `orders`, `accounts`.
*   **Groups by**: Aggregate metric (can be rolled up by day or location).
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`. Exclude `orders.status IN ('CANCELLED', 'REJECTED')`.
*   **Value definitions**:
    *   *Number of Orders*: `COUNT(orders.id)`
    *   *Average Ticket*: `SUM(accounts.total) / COUNT(orders.id)`

### 1.7 Payment Method Breakdown
*   **Reads tables**: `payments`.
*   **Groups by**: `payments.method` (e.g., CASH, CARD).
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`. Only include successful payment statuses.
*   **Value definitions**:
    *   *Total Collected*: `SUM(payments.amount)`

### 1.8 Discounts Given
*   **Reads tables**: Deferred until the `account_discounts` schema is finalized. 
*   *Note: The exact schema for `account_discounts` is not yet present in the `db/foundation-migrations-draft/` or the orders module migrations. Once implemented, this endpoint will query the discounts table grouped by discount type/name, showing the total count applied and the total monetary value.*

### 1.9 Voids and Cancellations
*   **Reads tables**: `cancellations_and_voids`.
*   **Groups by**: `operation_type` ('VOID' vs 'CANCEL'), and optionally by `reason`.
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`.
*   **Value definitions**: 
    *   *Count*: `COUNT(id)`
    *   *Value*: `SUM(amount)`

### 1.10 Refunds
*   **Reads tables**: `refunds`.
*   **Groups by**: `reason` (or aggregate total).
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`.
*   **Value definitions**: 
    *   *Count*: `COUNT(id)`
    *   *Value*: `SUM(amount)`

### 1.11 Tips Collected
*   **Reads tables**: `payments`.
*   **Groups by**: Aggregate total (optionally roll up by `visits.staff_id` by joining `accounts` and `visits`).
*   **Filters**: `location_id`, `from`/`to`. Only successful payments.
*   **Value definitions**: 
    *   *Total Tips*: `SUM(payments.tip_amount)`

## 2. Shared Query-Parameter Convention

All reporting endpoints MUST accept the following standard query parameters to ensure consistency across the API:

*   **`location_id` (UUID, optional)**: The specific location to report on. 
    *   If provided, the user must have `reports.sales.read` (or `reports.audit.read`) for this location.
    *   If omitted, the report aggregates data across "all locations the user has access to", matching the permission scoping precedent established by `orders.visits.read_all`. The backend will dynamically inject a `WHERE location_id IN (...)` clause based on the user's location-scoped role assignments.
*   **`from` and `to` (ISO 8601 DateTime strings, required)**: The time boundary for the report. The backend must convert these to the location's specific timezone when grouping by day or hour.
*   **`limit` and `offset` (Integer, optional)**: Required for reports that could return unbounded rows (e.g., Sales by Product). 
*   **`format` (String, optional)**: Accepts `json` (default) or `csv`.

## 3. Core Correctness Rules

These business logic constraints are critical to prevent silently incorrect financial reporting:

*   **Timezones**: Time-bucketed reports (sales by day, sales by hour) MUST use the location's own timezone (`locations.timezone`), never UTC. Aggregating by UTC hour will fundamentally misrepresent a physical business day.
*   **Voids and Cancellations**: A voided or cancelled order line (`order_lines.status IN ('VOIDED', 'CANCELLED')`) MUST NOT count toward gross sales, category totals, or product quantity counts. 
*   **Refunds vs. Net/Gross**: A refunded payment (`refunds` table) MUST NOT silently disappear from the "payments taken" totals. It must be reflected as a distinct "Refunds" metric so that Gross Sales and Net Sales are not improperly conflated. Net Sales = Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds.

---

## 4. Export Mechanism

The functional specification (`idea.md` section 28) explicitly mandates that reports must be exportable. 
*   This will be implemented via a simple `?format=csv` query parameter on any reporting REST endpoint.
*   When `format=csv` is requested, the Fastify server executes the exact same database query used for the JSON response but streams the output through a CSV stringifier, setting the `Content-Type: text/csv` and `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="report.csv"` headers.
*   This ensures the CSV export and the JSON dashboard numbers always perfectly match, providing a concrete, REST-appropriate mechanism without deferring exports as vague future work.

## 5. Explicitly Deferred Functionality

To strictly adhere to the Foundation scope constraints, the following analytical features are deliberately **OUT OF SCOPE** and must not be built into this reporting module:

1.  **Inventory Costing**: Theoretical-vs-actual inventory cost comparisons and COGS reporting (requires the future Inventory module).
2.  **Labor Cost & Hours**: Employee hours/labor-cost reporting (no shift/clock-in data model exists yet).
3.  **Customer-Level Reporting**: Top spenders, loyalty points, and purchase history by guest (requires the future Customer/Loyalty module).
4.  **Complex Multi-Location BI**: Advanced roll-up dashboards across organizations beyond the simple `location_id` filtering (single or "all accessible").
